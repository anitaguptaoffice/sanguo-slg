`FirstCityKnowledge.head` (via `reduceFirstCityKnowledge`). Local-only intents, conflicting evidence,
incomplete heads, and stale-actor drift withhold the display. No v1 `createCity`, no main-city navigation,
no playable-first-city or production-backend claims.
