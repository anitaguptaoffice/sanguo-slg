# First-city retained knowledge

CLIENT-FIRST-CITY-KNOWLEDGE-001 (#2509) adds a pure reducer, not an executable controller.
Start with createFirstCityKnowledge(fullScope); pass validated codec/Gateway/journal evidence to reduceFirstCityKnowledge.
Accumulate snapshots on the returned value. UNKNOWN is an empty evidence update, never fabricated rejection.
Inputs must already be strictly decoded and correlated to the authenticated request; this module does not authenticate.
Local proposals and canonical records remain separate, including a losing proposal or same-ID content conflict.
Pending can advance to a terminal; terminal receipts, C1 and template references cannot be overwritten.
Predecessors and same-response historical rejection/head pairs order heads without arrival-order assumptions.
Exact predecessor chains stay contiguous; historical ordering cannot insert an ancestor between adjacent commands.
Disconnected heads yield incomplete and no selected head until validated lineage connects them.
Contradictions, foreign scope input and journal unavailable are sticky; retained evidence remains readable.
head=null means unknown, incomplete or unavailable, not EMPTY. An observed EMPTY is never submission eligibility.
No output authorizes Submit, Continue or current city access. No UUID, I/O, storage, retry or navigation occurs.
The structural evidence shape fits the separate journal, without importing or enabling its unmerged implementation.
Future UI must guard session/view lifetime, display actual winner content, and require separate explicit Continue.
Actual V2 backend/source admission, durable ledger and current-owner city entry checks remain separate gates.
Pure codec/reducer tests and client build do not establish native storage, gameplay or database acceptance.
