# Daily task selection

Task titles are native buttons. Tab reaches the titles, and Enter or Space
selects the corresponding details. The selected title exposes its pressed
state. The row remains selectable with the pointer.

Refreshing a visible panel keeps focus on the same title when possible.
When that row disappears, focus moves to the active filter. Report and mail
links retain their own actions, and selecting a title only reads its details.
Skinned rows place progress and rewards below the title so they cannot
compress its focus target inside the fixed-height header.
