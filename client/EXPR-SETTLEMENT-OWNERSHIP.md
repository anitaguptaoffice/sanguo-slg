# Training settlement ownership

CLIENT-EXPR-SETTLEMENT-OWNER-001, issue #2055. Cancel, Finish and Faster are non-economic settlement commands: existing server handlers update hero status/EXP and delete the training session; only Begin charges gold. Begin dispatch/deduction, unknown wallet reconciliation, cultivation and largess are unchanged and outside this fix.

Previously selecting hero B while A's settlement was pending combined A's receipt ID with B's name, level and EXP baseline, also corrupting A's list summary. Settlement now captures actor, original hero/detail and presentation generation. Actor-owned success clears A's training snapshot and updates only A's status/EXP list fields. It does not overwrite unrelated level/loyalty changes. Current matching detail receives the authoritative total EXP (existing monotonic/idempotent helper); another hero's detail, timer and feedback are untouched. A newer same-hero detail intent is refreshed read-only after settlement instead of applying an older view baseline. No command is canceled or resent.

Training snapshots carry actor ownership, preventing a cached timer from leaking across relogin/account changes. Existing global training single-flight remains in force, including during the read-only same-hero follow-up. A late error cannot mark another hero's view as failed. Owned results can still be retained after closing/detaching without presenting stale feedback. Invalid receipt IDs are rejected.

Actual GetHeroDetail and ListPlayerHeroes encoding do not supply authoritative training status/timer, regardless of extra client-schema fields. This change does not claim to recover training after full reload or fix Begin's unresolved gold-reconciliation boundary. Mounted tests reproduce all three commands after a normal Begin, plus actor, lifecycle, newer-detail, current controls and no extra wallet patch. Full suite/build, independent review, #2004 disposable combination and exact-head/main CI remain gates. No native/live/deployment or hold release claim.
